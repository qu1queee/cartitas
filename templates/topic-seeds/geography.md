# Geography seeds

## Subtopics
- continents — seven big land areas on Earth
- oceans — five large bodies of salt water
- countries — nations, capitals, flags (start local, then expand)

## Early ideas
- Name the continents
- Which ocean is biggest?
- What country do we live in?
- What is a capital city?

## Middle ideas
- Why is Africa hot in the middle?
- What is an island?
- How is a country different from a continent?

## Cloze ideas
- There are [seven] continents.
- The Pacific is the biggest [ocean].
