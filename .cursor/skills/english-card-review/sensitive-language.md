# Non-sensitive language (language police)

Cartitas cards are for kids. English text must stay **non-sensitive**: calm, factual, and safe to read with a parent.

## Fail (do not approve)

Reject the English file if it includes any of the following, even as a "joke":

- Sexual content, innuendo, or adult body/relationship topics
- Slurs, hate speech, or insults aimed at a group or a child
- Graphic violence, gore, weapons used to hurt people, or cruelty to animals presented as entertainment
- Self-harm, suicide, or eating-disorder content
- Illegal drugs, getting drunk, or smoking as something to try
- Swearing and crude insults
- Scary horror imagery meant to frighten (monsters eating kids, torture, etc.)
- Discrimination or stereotypes presented as fact
- Personal data (real kids' names with locations, phone numbers, addresses)

Science, animals, sports, geography, and space may mention death, bones, predators, or war **only** at a calm, age-fit level (for example: "Some dinosaurs ate plants. Some ate other dinosaurs."). If the wording is graphic or sensational, fail it.

## Pass

- Plain facts, naming, and gentle why/compare questions
- Mild humor that does not punch down
- Age-band words from `templates/card-style.md`

When unsure, fail and quote the line. Kids' decks default to caution.
