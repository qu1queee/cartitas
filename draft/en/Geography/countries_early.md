# Countries — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: geography | subtopic: countries -->

Q: What is a country?
A: A country is a land with its own name, people, and rules. Brazil, Japan, and Kenya are countries.

---

C: Many countries have a [flag] with special colors and shapes.

---

Q: What is a border?
A: A border is where one country ends and the next country begins. Maps show borders as lines.

---

Q: Do all countries have the same weather?
A: No. Some countries are hot most of the year, and some are cold or snowy. It depends on where they are on Earth.

---

Q: Can lots of people live in one country?
A: Yes. A country can have millions of people in many cities and towns. Some countries have fewer people and more open land.
