# Oceans — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: geography | subtopic: oceans -->

Q: How many oceans are there on Earth?
A: Five. They are the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, and Arctic Oceans.

---

Q: Why is ocean water salty?
A: Rivers wash minerals from rocks into the sea. Over a long time, those minerals build up as salt in the ocean.

---

C: The cold ocean around Antarctica is called the [Southern] Ocean.

---

Q: How is a sea different from an ocean?
A: A sea is usually smaller and often partly enclosed by land. An ocean is a huge open body of salt water that covers large parts of Earth.

---

Q: Why do oceans matter for weather on land?
A: Oceans store heat from the Sun and move it around with currents. That helps shape rain, wind, and temperature far from the shore.
