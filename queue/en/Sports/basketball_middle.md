# Basketball — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: sports | subtopic: basketball -->

Q: What is an assist in basketball?
A: When one player passes the ball to a teammate who scores right away, the passer gets an assist. Sharing the ball helps the whole team score more.

---

C: If a player runs with the ball without [dribbling], the referee calls a traveling violation and the other team gets the ball.

---

Q: What is a timeout in basketball?
A: A coach can stop the clock for a short break called a timeout. Players catch their breath, drink water, and hear the plan for the next few plays.

---

Q: What is the painted rectangle under the basket called?
A: That area is often called the paint or the key. Many points come from shots taken close to the hoop inside that zone.
