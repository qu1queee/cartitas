# Basketball — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: sports | subtopic: basketball -->

Q: What color is a basketball usually?
A: Orange with black lines. That makes it easy to see when it moves fast.

---

C: You score in basketball by throwing the ball through the [hoop].

---

Q: Where do people play basketball?
A: On a court with hoops at each end. Many games happen in a gym or at an outdoor park.

---

Q: What hangs below the basketball ring?
A: A net. It catches the ball when someone scores so it does not fall straight to the floor.
