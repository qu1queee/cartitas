# Football — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: sports | subtopic: football -->

Q: Who guards the goal in football?
A: The goalkeeper. They try to stop the other team from scoring.

---

C: Only the [goalkeeper] may use their hands to stop the ball during a football match.

---

Q: What does a referee do in football?
A: The referee makes sure players follow the rules. They blow a whistle and can give yellow or red cards for fouls.

---

C: Players pass the ball to their [teammates] to move it up the field together.
