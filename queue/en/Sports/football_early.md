# Football — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: sports | subtopic: football -->

Q: What is the grassy field called where people play football?
A: It is called the pitch in many countries. In other places people say soccer field or football field.

---

C: Many football players wear [shin guards] under their socks to protect their legs.

---

Q: What happens at half-time in a football match?
A: Players take a short break between the two halves. They rest, drink water, and listen to their coach.

---

Q: Who wears a captain's armband on a football team?
A: The team captain wears it. The captain helps lead the team and may speak with the referee.
