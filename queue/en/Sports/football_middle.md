# Football — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: sports | subtopic: football -->

Q: What is a header in football?
A: A player hits the ball with their head to pass or shoot. Headers are useful when the ball flies high near the goal.

---

Q: What happens when a player gets a red card?
A: The player must leave the field right away. Their team plays with one fewer player for the rest of the match.

---

C: A [substitute] can come on from the bench to replace a tired or injured player.

---

Q: Why is the sport called "soccer" in the United States?
A: "Soccer" comes from an old short name for association football. In much of the world people say "football," but both names mean the same game.
