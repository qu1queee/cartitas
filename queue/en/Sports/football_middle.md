Q: What is a throw-in in football?
A: When the ball goes completely out over the sideline, the other team throws it back in with both hands from behind their head. Their feet must stay on the ground.

---

Q: What is the FIFA World Cup?
A: It is a huge football tournament where national teams from many countries compete. It happens every four years, and fans all over the world cheer for their country.
