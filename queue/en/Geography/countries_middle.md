Q: Why do some countries share the same money?
A: Neighboring countries sometimes use one currency to make trade simpler. Many countries in Europe use the euro, for example.

---

Q: What is a hemisphere?
A: A hemisphere is half of Earth. The equator is an imaginary line that splits the Northern Hemisphere from the Southern Hemisphere.

---

Q: What are the Alps?
A: The Alps are a long mountain range in Europe. They cross several countries, including France, Italy, and Switzerland.
