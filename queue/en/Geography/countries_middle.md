# Countries — middle (8–11)

<!-- lang: en | age: middle | topic: geography | subtopic: countries -->

Q: What is a capital city?
A: A capital is the main city where a country's government usually works. For example, Madrid is the capital of Spain.

---

Q: What is an island?
A: An island is land with water all around it. Ireland and Japan are islands.

---

C: A [country] is a place with its own name and rules, like France or Brazil.
