# Oceans — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: geography | subtopic: oceans -->

Q: What is a tsunami?
A: A tsunami is a very powerful wave, usually caused by an earthquake under the ocean. The water can rush far onto land when it reaches the shore.

---

Q: What is an iceberg?
A: A huge chunk of ice that broke off from a glacier and floats in the ocean. Most of an iceberg is hidden underwater.

Q: What is the difference between an ocean and a sea?
A: An ocean is one of the world's five huge salt-water bodies. A sea is smaller and often partly surrounded by land, like the Mediterranean Sea.

---

Q: Why do scientists study the ocean floor?
A: They map mountains, trenches, and volcanoes under the water. Learning about the seafloor helps us understand earthquakes and how Earth changes.
