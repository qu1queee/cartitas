# Oceans — middle (8–11)

<!-- lang: en | age: middle | topic: geography | subtopic: oceans -->

Q: How many oceans are there on Earth?
A: Five: the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, and Arctic Oceans. They all connect, so some scientists call them one world ocean.

---

C: About [71 percent] of Earth's surface is covered by ocean water.

---

Q: Why is ocean water salty?
A: Rivers carry tiny bits of rock and salt into the sea. Over millions of years the salt built up. The sun evaporates water but leaves the salt behind.

---

Q: What is the deepest part of the ocean?
A: The Mariana Trench in the Pacific Ocean. It is about 36,000 feet (11,000 meters) deep — deeper than Mount Everest is tall.

---

Q: What is a coral reef?
A: A coral reef is an underwater home built by tiny animals called corals. Reefs shelter fish and help protect coastlines from big waves.
