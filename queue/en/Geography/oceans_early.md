# Oceans — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: geography | subtopic: oceans -->

Q: What is salt water?
A: Water that has salt in it. Almost all ocean water is salt water, which is why it tastes salty.

---

C: Rain and melting snow flow in [rivers] until they reach the ocean.

---

Q: What is a coast?
A: The place where land meets the ocean. Beaches and cliffs are often found along the coast.

---

Q: Which ocean is around Antarctica?
A: The Southern Ocean. It is very cold and has icy winds.

---

C: The ocean touches every [continent] on Earth.
