Q: What is the difference between a lamb and a sheep?
A: A lamb is a young sheep, usually less than a year old. When it grows up, we call it a sheep, or a ewe if it is female.

---

Q: Why might a farmer collect chicken eggs every day?
A: Hens lay eggs whether or not a rooster is nearby. Collecting eggs often keeps them clean and stops hens from sitting on them to hatch chicks when the farmer wants eggs for food.
