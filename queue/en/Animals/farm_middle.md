# Farm animals — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: animals | subtopic: farm -->

Q: What is a turkey on a farm?
A: A turkey is a large bird that farmers raise for meat. Male turkeys can spread their tail feathers into a big fan.

---

Q: Why do farmers put fences around fields and pastures?
A: Fences keep cows, horses, and sheep from wandering onto roads. They also protect crops from animals that might eat them.

---

C: Many farmers sell fresh [vegetables] like tomatoes and carrots at a market in town on weekends.

---

Q: What does a beekeeper do on a farm?
A: A beekeeper cares for beehives and collects honey. Healthy bees also fly to flowers in nearby orchards and gardens, helping fruit grow.

---

Q: What is a scarecrow, and why might you see one in a field?
A: A scarecrow is a stuffed figure dressed in old clothes on a stick. Farmers hope it will scare birds away from seeds and growing crops.
