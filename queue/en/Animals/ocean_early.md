# Ocean animals — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: animals | subtopic: ocean -->

Q: What do many huge whales eat?
A: Tiny ocean animals and small fish. Whales gulp water and filter out their food.

---

C: You might see a [shark] fin sticking up above the waves when it swims near the surface.

---

Q: What is seaweed?
A: Seaweed is a plant that grows in the ocean. Fish and little sea creatures hide among its wavy leaves.

---

Q: Where do starfish usually live?
A: On the ocean floor, on rocks or sand. They move slowly on tiny tube feet under their arms.

---

C: Bright orange [clownfish] often swim safely among the waving tentacles of a sea anemone.
