Q: Why do cats have whiskers?
A: Whiskers are long, stiff hairs that help cats feel nearby objects, even in the dark. They warn a cat if a space is too tight to fit through.

Q: Why should you never leave a dog alone in a hot car?
A: Cars heat up very fast, even with windows cracked. A dog can get dangerously overheated in just a few minutes.
