# Pets — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: animals | subtopic: pets -->

Q: Why do dogs need walks every day?
A: Walks give dogs exercise and a chance to go to the bathroom outside. Sniffing on walks also keeps their mind busy and happy.

---

Q: What does a veterinarian do?
A: A veterinarian is a doctor for animals. They check pets when they are sick and help keep them healthy with vaccines and checkups.

---

Q: Why do cats have whiskers?
A: Whiskers are long, stiff hairs that help cats feel nearby objects, even in the dark. They warn a cat if a space is too tight to fit through.

Q: Why should you never leave a dog alone in a hot car?
A: Cars heat up very fast, even with windows cracked. A dog can get dangerously overheated in just a few minutes.
