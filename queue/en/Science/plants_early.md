# Plants — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: science | subtopic: plants -->

Q: What is soil?
A: The dirt where plants grow. It holds water and gives roots a place to grab on.

---

C: A [cactus] stores water in its thick stem so it can live in dry places.

---

Q: Do all plants need the same amount of sunlight?
A: No. Some like lots of sun, and some grow better in shade under trees.

---

Q: What is a sprout?
A: A tiny new plant that just popped up from a seed. It has small leaves and a short stem.

---

C: When you water a wilted houseplant, the leaves often stand up again because the plant drank the [water].
