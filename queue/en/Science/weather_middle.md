Q: What is a drought?
A: A drought is a long time with much less rain than usual. Rivers and lakes can shrink, and plants and farms may need extra water.

---

Q: Why can you sometimes see your breath on a cold morning?
A: Your warm breath holds water vapor. When it hits the cold air, the vapor turns into tiny drops that look like a little cloud.
