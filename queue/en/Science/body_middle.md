# Your body — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: science | subtopic: body -->

Q: What do your kidneys do?
A: Kidneys filter your blood and turn waste into urine. They also help control how much water stays in your body.

---

Q: Why do you blink without thinking about it?
A: Blinking spreads tears that keep your eyes wet and clean. It also gives your eyes a quick rest from bright light.

---

C: Your [ribs] curve around your chest and help protect your heart and lungs from bumps.

---

Q: What is your pulse?
A: Your pulse is the beat you feel when blood pumps through your arteries. It usually matches how fast your heart is beating.

---

Q: Why does your body need sleep?
A: Sleep gives your brain and muscles time to rest and repair. It also helps you remember what you learned during the day.
