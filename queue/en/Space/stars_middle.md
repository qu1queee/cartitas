Q: Why do stars look white or different colors?
A: Stars come in different temperatures. Hotter stars can look bluish-white. Cooler stars look more yellow or red.

---

Q: How many stars might be in the Milky Way galaxy?
A: Scientists think there are hundreds of billions of stars. That is so many that no one could count them all by hand.

---

Q: Why is it hard to see faint stars in a city at night?
A: Streetlights and car lights brighten the sky. This light pollution hides dimmer stars, so you see fewer of them than in the countryside.
