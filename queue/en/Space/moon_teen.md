# The Moon — teen (12+)

<!-- age: teen | lang: en | topic: space | subtopic: moon -->

Q: Is the far side of the Moon always dark?
A: No. It gets sunlight just like the side we see. It is called the far side because it always faces away from Earth, not because it stays dark.

---

Q: How fast is the Moon moving away from Earth?
A: About 1.5 inches (4 centimeters) per year. The Moon's gravity pulls on ocean tides, and that slowly nudges the Moon into a slightly bigger orbit.

---

C: Loose gray dust on the Moon's surface is called [regolith]. Apollo astronauts said it felt like sticky powder on their boots.

---

Q: What are moonquakes?
A: Shakes in the Moon's rocky crust. Instruments left by Apollo astronauts recorded them. They are usually weaker than most earthquakes on Earth.

---

Q: Why does the Moon sometimes look extra big near the horizon?
A: It is mostly an illusion in our eyes and brain. The Moon is not really closer at the horizon. A photo taken at the same time shows it is about the same size.
