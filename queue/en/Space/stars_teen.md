C: Astronomers measure how far away stars are using units called [light years]. One light year is how far light travels in one year.

---

Q: What happens when a very big star runs out of fuel?
A: The core collapses and the star can explode in a supernova. That blast can create heavy elements and sometimes leave behind a neutron star or a black hole.

---

Q: What is a nebula?
A: A nebula is a cloud of gas and dust in space. Some nebulas are where new stars are born. Others are leftover material from old stars that died.

---

Q: Can planets orbit stars other than the Sun?
A: Yes. Planets around other stars are called exoplanets. Scientists have found thousands of them by watching stars wobble slightly or dim when a planet passes in front.
