Q: Why does the Moon look bright at night?
A: It does not make its own light. Sunlight bounces off the Moon and reaches our eyes.

---

Q: What is a crescent Moon?
A: A thin curved slice of the Moon. You see it when only a small part is lit by the Sun.

---

Q: How many moons does Earth have?
A: One. Our Moon is the bright round object we see in the night sky.
