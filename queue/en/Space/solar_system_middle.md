# Solar system — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: space | subtopic: solar_system -->

Q: What is Jupiter's Great Red Spot?
A: A giant storm in Jupiter's clouds. It has been swirling for hundreds of years and is bigger than Earth.

---

C: A [comet] is an icy object in space that can grow a bright tail when it passes close to the Sun.

---

Q: Why do scientists send rovers to Mars?
A: Mars had water long ago. Rovers study rocks and soil to learn whether tiny life could have lived there in the past.

---

Q: Which planet takes the shortest time to orbit the Sun?
A: Mercury. It goes around the Sun in only about 88 Earth days.

---

C: Almost all of the mass in our solar system is in the [Sun].
