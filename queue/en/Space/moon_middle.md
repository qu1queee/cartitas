# The Moon — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: space | subtopic: moon -->

Q: What color does the Moon usually look from Earth?
A: Mostly gray. Its surface is dusty rock that bounces sunlight back to us.

---

Q: What are craters on the Moon?
A: Round bowl-shaped scars left when rocks from space crash into the Moon. Some craters are wider than a city.

---

Q: What is a full Moon?
A: When we see the whole bright face of the Moon lit by the Sun. It happens about once a month in the Moon's cycle.

---

C: During a [new Moon], the Moon is between Earth and the Sun, so the side facing us looks dark.

---

Q: Can you hear sounds on the Moon?
A: Not outside a spacesuit. The Moon has almost no air, and sound needs air to travel.
