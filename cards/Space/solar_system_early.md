# Solar system — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | topic: space | subtopic: solar_system -->

Q: What star does Earth go around?
A: The Sun. One trip around the Sun takes one year.

---

C: [Mars] is called the red planet because it looks reddish in the sky.

---

Q: Which planet is famous for its rings?
A: Saturn. Its rings are made of ice and rock.
