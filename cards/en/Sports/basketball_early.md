# Basketball — early (5–7)

<!-- lang: en | age: early | topic: sports | subtopic: basketball -->

Q: Where do you shoot the ball to score in basketball?
A: Into the hoop above the court.

---

C: You [dribble] the ball by bouncing it on the floor while you move.

---

Q: Is it okay to hold the ball and run without dribbling?
A: No. That is called traveling and the other team gets the ball.
