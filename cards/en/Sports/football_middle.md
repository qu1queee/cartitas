Q: What does "offside" mean in football?
A: You are offside if you are closer to the other team's goal than both the ball and the last defender when a teammate passes to you. The rule stops attackers from just waiting by the goal.

Q: Why does the goalkeeper wear a different colored shirt?
A: So everyone can tell the keeper apart from the other players. Only the goalkeeper may use their hands inside the penalty area to stop shots.

# Football — middle (8–11)

Q: When does the referee award a penalty kick?
A: If a player fouls an opponent inside their own penalty area, the other team gets a penalty kick. One player shoots from the penalty spot with only the goalkeeper trying to stop it.

---

C: If the ball crosses the goal line after a defender last touched it, the attacking team takes a [corner] kick from the corner flag.

---

Q: What is a throw-in in football?
A: When the ball goes completely out over the sideline, the other team throws it back in with both hands from behind their head. Their feet must stay on the ground.

---

Q: What is the FIFA World Cup?
A: It is a huge football tournament where national teams from many countries compete. It happens every four years, and fans all over the world cheer for their country.
