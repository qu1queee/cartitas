# Olympics — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: sports | subtopic: olympics -->

Q: What colors are the five Olympic rings?
A: Blue, yellow, black, green, and red. Each ring is a different color.

---

C: The Olympic Games take place every [four] years.

---

Q: What do athletes win at the Olympics?
A: Medals. The best athletes can win gold, silver, or bronze.

---

Q: Who takes part in the Olympic Games?
A: Athletes from many countries around the world. They travel to one city to compete in sports.
