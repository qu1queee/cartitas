# Plants — early (5–7)

<!-- lang: en | age: early | topic: science | subtopic: plants -->

Q: What three things do most plants need to grow?
A: Water, light, and air.

C: A new plant often starts from a [seed].

Q: Why are leaves usually green?
A: They have a substance called chlorophyll that helps the plant make food from sunlight.
