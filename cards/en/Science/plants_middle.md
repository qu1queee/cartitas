# Plants — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: science | subtopic: plants -->

Q: What is photosynthesis?
A: It is how plants make their own food. They use sunlight, water, and air to build sugar that helps them grow.

---

Q: Why do people and animals need plants to breathe?
A: During photosynthesis, plants release oxygen into the air. We breathe in that oxygen to stay alive.

---

C: In photosynthesis, plants take in carbon dioxide and give off [oxygen].

---

Q: What do roots do for a plant?
A: Roots hold the plant in the soil and soak up water and minerals. They also store some of the food the plant makes.
