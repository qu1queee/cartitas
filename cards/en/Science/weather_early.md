# Weather — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: science | subtopic: weather -->

Q: What makes rain?
A: Tiny drops of water in clouds grow bigger and heavier until they fall to the ground.

---

C: When the air is very cold, rain can freeze and fall as [snow].

---

Q: How many seasons are there in one year?
A: Four: spring, summer, fall, and winter.

---

Q: What do you use to stay dry in the rain?
A: An umbrella or a raincoat keeps rain off your clothes.
