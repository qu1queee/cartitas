# Continents — early (5–7)

<!-- lang: en | age: early | topic: geography | subtopic: continents -->

Q: How many continents are there?
A: Seven. They are Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania, and South America.

---

C: [Africa] is a continent with deserts, jungles, and big animals like elephants.

---

Q: Which continent do penguins live on in the wild?
A: Antarctica. It is very cold and covered in ice.

# Continents — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: geography | subtopic: continents -->

Q: What is a continent?
A: A continent is a very big piece of land on Earth. There are seven continents in all.

---

C: [Asia] is the biggest continent on Earth.

---

Q: Which continent has the Sahara Desert?
A: Africa. The Sahara is a huge hot desert in the north of Africa.

---

Q: Which continent has kangaroos in the wild?
A: Oceania. Kangaroos live in Australia, which is part of the continent of Oceania.

---

C: North America and South America are two separate [continents].

---

# Continents — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: geography | subtopic: continents -->

Q: Which continent has the United States, Canada, and Mexico?
A: North America. All three countries are on the same continent.

---

Q: On which continent can you see the pyramids of Egypt?
A: Africa. The pyramids were built long ago near the Nile River.
