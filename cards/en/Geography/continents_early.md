# Continents — early (5–7)

<!-- lang: en | age: early | topic: geography | subtopic: continents -->

Q: How many continents are there?
A: Seven. They are Africa, Antarctica, Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania, and South America.

---

C: [Africa] is a continent with deserts, jungles, and big animals like elephants.

---

Q: Which continent do penguins live on in the wild?
A: Antarctica. It is very cold and covered in ice.
