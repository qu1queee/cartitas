# Ocean animals — middle (8–11)

<!-- lang: en | age: middle | topic: animals | subtopic: ocean -->

Q: Are whales fish?
A: No. Whales are mammals. They breathe air and feed milk to their babies.

---

Q: Why do sharks have so many teeth?
A: Sharks lose teeth often and grow new ones. Many rows of teeth help them catch food in the ocean.

---

C: [Octopuses] have eight arms and can change color to hide.
