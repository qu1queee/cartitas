# Ocean animals — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: animals | subtopic: ocean -->

Q: Where do many fish, dolphins, and whales live?
A: In the ocean. The ocean is the huge salty sea that covers much of Earth.

---

C: Fish use their [fins] to swim.

---

Q: What does a sea turtle have on its back?
A: A hard shell. It is like a shield that protects it in the water.
