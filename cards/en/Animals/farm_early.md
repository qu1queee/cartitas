<!-- lang: en | age: early | topic: animals | subtopic: farm -->

Q: What do cows give us that we drink?
A: Milk. Milk can also be made into cheese and yogurt.

---

C: [Chickens] lay eggs that many people eat for breakfast.

Q: What sound does a sheep make?
A: Baa! Sheep live on farms and give us wool for warm clothes.

# Farm animals — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: animals | subtopic: farm -->

Q: What sound does a cow make?
A: Moo! Cows say moo when they talk to each other.

---

C: Baby chickens are called [chicks].

---

Q: What do farmers often use horses for?
A: Horses can pull carts, carry riders, and help with work on the farm.

---

Q: Where do ducks like to swim?
A: In ponds. Ducks have webbed feet that help them paddle through the water.

---

Q: What does a rooster do in the morning?
A: It crows loudly. Its call sounds like "cock-a-doodle-doo."
