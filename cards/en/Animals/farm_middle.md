Q: Why do pigs roll in mud?
A: Pigs do not sweat much, so a coat of mud cools them down and shields their skin from the sun. They are not trying to be messy — they are staying safe and comfortable.

Q: How is a horse's hoof different from a dog's paw?
A: A hoof is a hard covering that protects the horse's toe. Horses walk on one big toe on each foot, which helps them run fast on hard ground.

---

Q: Which farm animals are mammals?
A: Cows, pigs, horses, sheep, and goats are mammals. They have hair or fur, breathe air, and feed milk to their babies. Chickens are birds, not mammals.
