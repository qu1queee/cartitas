# Solar system — teen (12+)

<!-- lang: en | age: teen | topic: space | subtopic: solar_system -->

Q: Why is Pluto not called a planet anymore?
A: In 2006, astronomers made a clearer rule: a planet must clear other objects from its orbit. Pluto shares its zone with many similar icy bodies, so it is called a dwarf planet.

---

Q: What is the asteroid belt?
A: A ring of rocky objects between Mars and Jupiter. Most asteroids stay there and do not hit planets.
