# The Moon — teen (12+)

<!-- age: teen | lang: en | topic: space | subtopic: moon -->

Q: How did the Moon probably form?
A: Scientists think a Mars-sized object hit early Earth about 4.5 billion years ago. Debris flew into orbit and clumped together to make the Moon.

---

Q: Does the Moon have air or weather like Earth?
A: No. The Moon has almost no atmosphere. Without air, temperatures swing wildly between day and night, and there is no wind or rain.

---

C: During a solar eclipse, the [Moon] passes between Earth and the Sun and blocks sunlight for a short time.

---

Q: Is there water on the Moon?
A: Yes. In some permanently shadowed craters near the poles, water is frozen as ice. Future explorers might melt it for drinking or split it to make rocket fuel.

---

Q: What did Apollo astronauts bring back from the Moon?
A: Moon rocks and soil samples. Studying them showed the Moon is about 4.5 billion years old and helped scientists test ideas about how it formed.
