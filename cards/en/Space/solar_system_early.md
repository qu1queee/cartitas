Q: What star does Earth go around?
A: The Sun. One trip around the Sun takes one year.

---

C: [Mars] is called the red planet because it looks reddish in the sky.

---

Q: Which planet is famous for its rings?

# Solar system — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: space | subtopic: solar_system -->

Q: What is the solar system?
A: The Sun and everything that travels around it, like the planets and their moons.

---

C: Mercury and [Venus] are the two planets closest to the Sun.

---

Q: Which planet do we live on?
A: Earth. It is the third planet from the Sun.

---

Q: What keeps the planets from drifting away into space?
A: The Sun's gravity. It pulls on the planets and keeps them orbiting the Sun.

---

Q: Which planet is often called Earth's neighbor in space?
A: Mars. It is the next planet out from the Sun after Earth.
