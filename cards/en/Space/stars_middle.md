# Stars — middle (8–11)

<!-- lang: en | age: middle | topic: space | subtopic: stars -->

Q: Is the Sun a star?
A: Yes. The Sun is the closest star to Earth. It looks bigger because it is much closer than other stars.

---

Q: What is a galaxy?
A: A galaxy is a huge group of stars, gas, and dust. Earth is in the Milky Way galaxy.

---

C: Light from the Sun takes about [8 minutes] to reach Earth.

# Stars — middle (8–11)

<!-- age: middle | lang: en | topic: space | subtopic: stars -->

Q: What is the closest star to Earth besides the Sun?
A: Proxima Centauri. It is part of a group of three stars called Alpha Centauri. It is still trillions of miles away—much too far to visit in one lifetime.

---

C: A [shooting star] is not really a star. It is a small bit of rock or dust burning up in Earth's atmosphere.
