# The Moon — early (5–7)

<!-- lang: en | age: early | topic: space | subtopic: moon -->

Q: Why does the Moon look different shapes during the month?
A: We see different parts of the Moon lit by the Sun. That is why it looks full, half, or like a thin slice.

---

C: The [Moon] goes around Earth about once every month.

---

Q: Has anyone walked on the Moon?
A: Yes. Astronauts from NASA walked on the Moon in 1969. Twelve people have walked there in total.

# The Moon — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | lang: en | topic: space | subtopic: moon -->

Q: What is the Moon?
A: A big rocky ball that travels around Earth. It is much smaller than our planet.

---

C: At [night], we often see the Moon shining in the sky.
