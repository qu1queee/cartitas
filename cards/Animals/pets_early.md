# Pets — early (5–7)

Q: What do dogs often do when they are happy to see you?
A: They wag their tail and may jump or lick you.

---

C: A [cat] often purrs when it feels calm and happy.

Q: What should you always do before eating if you touched a pet?
A: Wash your hands with soap and water.
