# Farm animals — early (5–7)

<!-- age: early | topic: animals | subtopic: farm -->

Q: What do cows give us that we drink?
A: Milk. Milk can also be made into cheese and yogurt.

---

C: [Chickens] lay eggs that many people eat for breakfast.

---

Q: What sound does a sheep make?
A: Baa! Sheep live on farms and give us wool for warm clothes.
